## PART 2 — THE PART I ACTUALLY WORK ON

**1. OJK's BNPL rules turn income verification into a defined, auditable workflow**
Effective July 1: BNPL borrowers must be 18+ or married, prove ≥Rp3M/month gross income via payslips or bank statements through an "approved income prediction method," and lenders must cap debt payments at a set share of income and screen for exposure across 3+ paylater providers. — [Fintech News Indonesia](https://fintechnews.id/110330/lending/ojk-indonesia-bnpl-paylater-regulations-limits/), [ABNR](https://www.abnrlaw.com/news/from-rapid-growth-to-institutional-maturity-a-look-at-ojks-new-regulation-on-buy-now-pay-later-bnpl)
*Operational read:* This is OJK codifying exactly the kind of document-based income verification and multi-lender exposure check that touches our onboarding and SME document collection. It's a signal of where OJK's KYC bar for lending broadly is heading, not just BNPL.
*One action:* Ask Compliance/Product whether our income-verification method would meet the "approved income prediction model" standard, and whether our applicant screening already checks multi-lender exposure the way OJK now mandates for BNPL.

**2. Branch speed is the CX metric nobody's dashboard is catching**
2026 CX benchmarking shows Customer Effort Score is now 1.8x more predictive of loyalty than CSAT and 2x more than NPS; task-speed scores for mobile banking lead (JPMorgan at 82%), while in-branch speed trails sharply across institutions. — [Resonate CX](https://www.resonate.cx/blog/customer-effort-score-ces/), [Sogolytics](https://www.sogolytics.com/blog/what-banking-customers-want-cx-rankings-2026/)
*Operational read:* If our measurement stack is still NPS-anchored, it's blind to the exact gap — branch/frontline effort — where our SAs operate and where friction is most likely concentrated.
*One action:* Check whether we run transactional, task-level CES for branch and SME-onboarding journeys specifically (not just relationship NPS), segmented by channel.

**3. SME lending's real friction is the handoff between KYB, director verification, and credit decisioning**
Industry analysis flags that treating entity verification, ownership/director checks, and credit scoring as separate steps — rather than one flow — is now the main friction point in SME lending, even as Indonesian fintechs compress KYB from days to seconds using digital public infrastructure. — [Voveid](https://blog.voveid.com/sme-lending-in-2026-kyb-director-verification-and-credit-decisioning-as-one-flow/)
*Operational read:* This describes our likely abandonment pattern: an applicant clears KYB, then stalls at director/ownership verification, then waits again for scoring — three separate systems instead of one flow.
*One action:* Map our SME application step-by-step and flag every point where KYB, director verification, and credit decisioning hand off between different systems or teams — that's the probable drop-off point.
