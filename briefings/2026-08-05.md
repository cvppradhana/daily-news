## PART 2 — THE PART I ACTUALLY WORK ON

**1. Agent-assist AI is becoming standard frontline kit, not a pilot novelty**
Bank of America is equipping reps with GenAI drafting tools; NAB is testing agentic AI guardrails; 37% of US bank contact centers already use GenAI, another 37% planning 2026 rollout. — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/bank-of-america-equips-customer-service-reps-with-gen-ai/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/nab-tests-ai-agents-in-plan-to-deploy-tech-to-banking-customers)
*Operational read:* This is squarely our lane — real-time suggested responses, policy surfacing, and call summarization for SAs. It also collides with OJK's emerging AI governance expectations (audit trail, explainability) referenced in Part 1.
*One action:* Check whether our agent-assist/knowledge-base tooling logs AI-suggested responses for audit, and whether an SA reply that originated from an AI suggestion needs disclosure under OJK's forthcoming AI governance guidance.

**2. SME lending abandonment is an industry-wide KYB/verification problem, not just an individual-onboarding one**
Industry analysis frames KYB, director/beneficial-owner verification, and credit decisioning as needing to run as one flow — fragmented handoffs are the leak. — [Voveid](https://blog.voveid.com/sme-lending-in-2026-kyb-director-verification-and-credit-decisioning-as-one-flow/)
*Operational read:* If our non-individual flow treats document collection, director verification, and scoring as separate stages with waiting between them, we're likely bleeding SME applicants at the same friction points the industry is now naming explicitly — just at higher value per drop-off.
*One action:* Pull the SME funnel and tag where drop-off concentrates — document collection, BO/director verification, or credit-decision wait time. Worth a slide on its own.

**3. IT governance regulation now directly covers internal frontline/onboarding systems**
POJK 1/2026 (Part 1) sets IT governance requirements for commercial banks' internal systems, not just customer-facing products.
*Operational read:* Our case management, SA desktop, and onboarding platforms likely fall under this regime — change management, incident response, and vendor-risk documentation may now be a formal requirement, not best practice.
*One action:* Confirm with Tech Risk/Compliance whether Assisted Channel tooling has been mapped against POJK 1/2026 yet, or whether that's still open.

**4. CX measurement is shifting away from NPS toward effort and resolution**
Customer Effort Score is now cited as 1.8x more predictive of loyalty than CSAT and 2x more than NPS; 2026 best practice is issue-type/channel-level monthly reporting. — [Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/customer-effort-score-statistics-2026)
*Operational read:* NPS at the end of a journey doesn't tell us what a specific SA interaction or tool cost the customer in effort — CES and FCR are the metrics that actually move when we fix tooling.
*One action:* Check whether our assisted-channel dashboards capture CES or FCR at the case level, or only end-of-journey NPS — flag the gap if it's NPS-only.
